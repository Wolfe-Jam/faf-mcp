FAF defines. AGENTS.md instructs. AI codes.

Define once. Sync everywhere.

You keep .cursorrules. A teammate keeps AGENTS.md. Someone just moved to Gemini. Every tool wants its own file, and they drift apart. With faf-mcp, the facts come from one place.

project.faf

Facts in one file

The stack, the goal and who it's for. Derived from your repo; the human slots are approved by you.

AGENTS.md · .cursorrules · GEMINI.md · CLAUDE.md

Written, not copied

faf-cli's own renderers write a managed block in each. Your own rules outside it stay byte for byte.

faf_score

Scored, not guessed

The always-33 score from faf-cli's kernel, the same number in the terminal and in your IDE. 100% is Trophy ✪.

Install in your IDE

It runs locally over stdio, so your files stay on your machine. No API key, no env vars required.

// add to your MCP config {"mcpServers": {"faf": { "command": "bunx", "args": ["faf-mcp"] }}}

No Bun? Use npx with ["-y", "faf-mcp"]. Node 22 or newer.
